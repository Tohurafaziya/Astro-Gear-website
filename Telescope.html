Product Description

The Celestron NexStar 8SE is our flagship computerized Schmidt-Cassegrain telescope, blending classic heritage with next-generation features. Featuring a large 8-inch aperture, it gathers enough light to reveal breathtaking details of the solar system and deep-sky objects. The iconic "orange tube" design has been updated with the latest fully automated GoTo mount technology.

Whether you are a seasoned astronomer or a passionate beginner, the NexStar 8SE makes night sky navigation effortless. The included NexStar+ hand control features a database of over 40,000 celestial objects. Simply use the SkyAlign technology to align your telescope on any three bright celestial objects, and you are ready to explore the universe in minutes.

Technical Specifications

  • Optical Design: Schmidt-Cassegrain
  • Aperture: 203.2mm (8 inches)
  • Focal Length: 2032mm (80 inches)
  • Focal Ratio: f/10
  • Mount Type: Computerized Altitude-Azimuth Single Fork Arm
  • Database: 40,000+ objects, 100 user-defined programmable objects
  • Total Kit Weight: 24 lbs (10.8 kg)
  • Included Accessories: 25mm eyepiece, StarPointer finderscope, NexStar+ hand control, steel tripod
